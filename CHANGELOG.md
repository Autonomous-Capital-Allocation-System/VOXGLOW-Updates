# VoxGlow release notes

## 0.1.0 - First beta

- One-window vocal repair and glow: LEARN, CLEAN, SMOOTH, BODY, FORWARD, AIR, MIX, OUTPUT.
- CLEAN: de-clip, de-crackle (with pitch-pulse protection and a phone/codec mode), denoise,
  room tame, plosive guard, and removal of crackle residue above a band-limited source.
- AIR regenerates missing highs on phone and band-limited vocals; BODY adds warmth above the
  source's low cut; FORWARD levels, compresses and adds presence.
- HEAR REMOVED / HEAR ADDED monitoring, before/after spectrum, auto gain match, -1 dBFS ceiling.
- Offline license activation; manual Check Updates.
